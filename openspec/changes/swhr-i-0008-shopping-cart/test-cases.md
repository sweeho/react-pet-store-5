### SWHR-R-0132 · Cart screen

#### SWHR-R-0132.01 · Empty cart is displayed
- **SWHR-C-0232** (unit, approved, v1) Empty cart screen shows empty message and no rows or controls
  - Preconditions: Cart page rendered in a MemoryRouter with an empty cart.
  - Step 1: Render the cart screen
  - Step 2: Query for the empty message, item rows, Update Cart, total and Check Out
  - Expected: 'Your Shopping Cart is Empty.' is shown; no item rows, Update Cart control, total or Check Out control exist.

#### SWHR-R-0132.02 · Cart with items is displayed
- **SWHR-C-0233** (unit, approved, v1) Cart with EST-6 x2 and EST-1 x1 shows rows, total $53.50 and controls
  - Preconditions: Cart page rendered with 2 x EST-6 'Adult Male Bulldog' at 18.50 and 1 x EST-1 'Large Angelfish' at 16.50.
  - Step 1: Render the cart screen
  - Step 2: Inspect each row and the footer
  - Expected: Two rows: each has attribute and name linked to the item detail page, a Remove control, quantity field (2 and 1), unit price ($18.50 and $16.50). Update Cart, total '$53.50' and Check Out are shown.

#### SWHR-R-0132.03 · Remove control is used
- **SWHR-C-0234** (e2e, approved, v1) Remove on EST-6 row removes it and redisplays the cart
  - Preconditions: App on :5178; cart holds EST-6 and EST-1.
  - Step 1: Open the cart screen
  - Step 2: Activate Remove on the EST-6 row
  - Expected: The cart screen is displayed again, EST-6 is gone and EST-1 remains.

#### SWHR-R-0132.04 · Update Cart is submitted
- **SWHR-C-0235** (e2e, approved, v1) Update Cart submits all quantities: EST-6 to 5, EST-1 to 0
  - Preconditions: App on :5178; cart holds EST-6 x2 and EST-1 x1; network requests recorded.
  - Step 1: Open the cart screen
  - Step 2: Set EST-6 quantity to 5 and EST-1 to 0
  - Step 3: Activate Update Cart
  - Expected: A single update request carries both quantities; the cart screen redisplays with EST-6 quantity 5 and no EST-1 row.

#### SWHR-R-0132.05 · Check Out is used
- **SWHR-C-0236** (e2e, approved, v1) Check Out starts the order information entry step
  - Preconditions: App on :5178; signed-on shopper with at least one item in the cart.
  - Step 1: Open the cart screen
  - Step 2: Activate Check Out
  - Expected: The order information entry page of checkout is shown.
